चम्पूल (Champul)

noun /ˈtʃʌm.puːl/ • Ancient Indian Board Game

Champul (चम्पूल) is a traditional Indian board game played on a 5×5 grid, using cowrie shells (kavadi) instead of dice. Roll the shells, move your four pieces around the board and into the centre, and knock your opponent back to start along the way. It’s simple to learn and full of lucky turns, and families have played versions of it for generations. Champul (चम्पूल) is a traditional Indian 5×5 board game played with cowrie shells. 📜 Rules of Champul (चम्पूल)

🎯 Objective

Guide all 4 of your pawns around the outer and inner circuits into the Central Goal (चम्पूल). The first player to reach the goal with all pawns wins!

🎲 Kavadi Points Reference
1 Open 1 Pt
2 Open 2 Pts
3 Open 3 Pts
4 Open 4 Pts
•
All 4 Closed (Ashta): Scores 8 Points and awards an immediate Bonus Turn!
🔄 Track & Movement
•
Outer Circuit: Pawns move Anti-Clockwise around the outer 16 boxes starting from your home station.
•
Inner Circuit: At your designated entry square (marked with an arrow), turn inward and navigate Clockwise through the inner ring.
•
Central Goal: Enter the Center Sanctum with an exact roll. Reaching the goal awards a Bonus Turn!
⚔️ Safe Havens & Capturing
•
Safe Squares ('X'): The 4 starting home squares, 4 inner corner squares, and the Center goal are safe zones where pawns can never be captured.
•
Capturing (Killing): Landing on an opponent pawn on any non-safe square captures it back to its starting home and earns you a Bonus Turn!
•
Inner Route Entry (>2 Players): In 3 or 4 player games, you must capture at least one opponent pawn before any of your pawns are allowed to enter the inner ring past box 12.
•
Friendly Rule: Two pawns of the same color cannot stay together on the same non-safe square.
